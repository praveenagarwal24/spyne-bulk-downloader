Spyne Bulk Media Downloader

Upload a CSV of media IDs, paste your Spyne credentials, hit Download. Calls api.spyne.ai/medias/bulk-download directly from your browser.

1. Your Spyne credentials

Open console.spyne.ai, log in, then open DevTools → Network tab. Trigger any action, click any request, and copy the values. They're saved locally in your browser.

2. Media IDs (CSV upload)

CSV must contain a column named Media ID (case-insensitive; also accepts mediaId, media_id). Optional columns: VIN / Sku Name for human-readable labels, plus Enterprise ID, Team ID, User ID to override the credentials in section 1 on a per-row basis.
